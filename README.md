## Hey, I'm Benjamin 

I'm a developer who enjoys turning ambitious ideas into fast, polished products. I build across the full stack, with a soft spot for **developer tools, community software, and interfaces that feel effortless**.

Currently building with **[Waveserv](https://github.com/waveserv-com)** and shipping projects from **[blanke.lol](https://blanke.lol)**.

## What I build

- **Developer tools** that make the boring parts of shipping disappear.
- **Web experiences** with a focus on speed, accessibility, and visual detail.
- **Community products** that help people connect without adding unnecessary friction.
